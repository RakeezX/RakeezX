1. **Stable academic identities:** Switching official presets keeps the same logical academic year, first and second terms, and Teaching IDs. A preset may change official date values, never these identities.
2. **Official term dates:** The switch updates official academic-year dates where applicable and both terms’ start/end dates, without making new term identities.
3. **Official references:** The selected preset controls official holidays, teaching/non-teaching reference days, Calendar temporal information, “أين نحن الآن؟”, next milestone and official term date presentation.
4. **Today-only operation:** A preset switch alone does not materialize, delete, regenerate or mutate future sessions. When generating today's missing sessions, check active term, today's date within current official/custom effective bounds, selected preset and today's effective Calendar kind before deciding eligibility.
5. **Historical immutability:** A later preset switch does not reinterpret, delete, cancel, recreate or rewrite persisted past sessions, attendance, scores, logs or other durable historical teaching records solely because the new preset classifies a past date differently.
6. **Account-synced choice:** Selected preset is account/year truth across authorized devices, not independent per-device preference. The technical foundation decides storage and Sync design.
7. **Preset-scoped customizations:** Teacher exceptions and customizations remain attached to their creating preset. Switching away preserves but does not apply them to an unrelated preset; switching back restores them. This especially matters for exceptions tied to official holidays that differ between presets.
8. **Official-holiday association:** Future design may use a stable official-event identity where an exception extends/modifies a known holiday; no final schema is selected here.
9. **Term-date customization:** For now, any supported teacher term-date customization is preset-scoped and restored when returning. A future centrally managed official-date publisher may supersede teacher-editable official dates, but is neither implemented nor authorized here.
10. **Education Administration:** Future إدارة التعليم profile data may auto-select the appropriate official preset; this is not required for N58's initial foundation.
11. **Teacher-controlled term transition:** Dates do not silently change `activeTermId`. If the second term's recorded start arrives while the first remains active, calmly say “الفصل الدراسي الثاني بدأ” and offer “تفعيل الفصل الدراسي الثاني” or “لاحقًا”. The teacher decides.
12. **Year/term settings UX:** Future Work should connect academic-year and term management in one coherent flow after the Codex runtime/data foundation; no UX implementation is included now.

The official Calendar catalog/admin publisher remains a separate **deferred** study: possible central official dates and Draft → Preview → Publish/rollback with Education Administration association. No publisher backend, schema or admin UI is approved by D-076.
