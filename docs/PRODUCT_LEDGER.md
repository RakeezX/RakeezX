## Structured ledger

Triage is by the Product Owner and ChatGPT PM. An INBOX note is **not** implementation authorization. Keep genuine resolved, rejected, and superseded Product items visible; change their status and Last Updated date rather than deleting them. The PO explicitly removed the initial bootstrap tracking rows in 0304. One item may reference multiple tickets. Archive capability atoms belong in [Recovery Ledger](RECOVERY_LEDGER.md), not here.
