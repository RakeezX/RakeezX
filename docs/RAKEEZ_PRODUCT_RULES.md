# RAKEEZ X Product Rules

- The Product Owner is the final authority for Product scope and acceptance.
- Git commits, exact patches, hashes, manifests, and recorded validation are engineering truth; chat history is not.
- Production runtime and data semantics are authoritative.
- A ticket is not `CLOSED` until the Product Owner verifies it in Production.
- Local, Work, Google Visual Lab, Preview, and test candidates are not Production truth.
- Real school data must never be copied into handoff packages, fixtures, screenshots, or test artifacts.
- Production promotion is selective and evidence-based. Unrelated changes do not travel with an approved ticket.
