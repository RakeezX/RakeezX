## Diagnosis and result

The original real-app baseline reproduced an ownerless `createTeaching` row whose Students paste button opened no sheet. Both Students and Gradebook setup demanded an explicit row owner. The actual Sync teaching:create ACK experiment also established that `decodeSyncV2Canonical` does **not** project owner_id into ownerId, so canonical receipt adoption can legitimately remain ownerless. Sync serialization/ACLs were left intact.

`store.assertClassOperationsAccess` now supplies the common access decision used by Students, hub actions, class-list koshoof entry and Gradebook/koshoof. It requires the current ready account workspace, matching runtime owner binding and selected database, matching authenticated user/session, persisted Auth identity through the existing lease guard, active term/year and nonarchived class. It rejects explicit owner conflicts on the teaching and its parents. Account DB provenance must match its immutable lease generation and the device claim, restore or provisioning receipt. Missing ownerId alone grants nothing. Existing write/Sync/server checks remain in place. Paste/add recheck access and the captured workspace after awaiting a sheet. Denied clicks show a concise warning.

The baseline also measured 12 complete application paints for 12 identical synthetic account notifications at 390px (paint count 6→18). `app.js` now skips presentation remounts when visible account/workspace/lease state is unchanged, while retaining account mismatch/deactivation handling and all notification delivery. Final measurement at each of 320/390/768/1280 was **one** paint across the 12 events (initial transition from the test's mounted Demo shell to the claimed account), with unchanged class-card x/y. Subsequent identical events do not repeatedly remount the view. First-click navigation also passed with a notification between pointerdown and pointerup.

This bounds the proved local remount defect. It does not claim reproduction or resolution of the user's full intermittent freeze, inspection of their browser, or acceptance of the separate N64 callback/freeze issue. No Auth callback/email-confirm code or denied N64 operation was changed/replayed.
