## Denial and privacy rules

The caller passes the mounted workspace, active raw year, year-wide teaching/session records and only their relevant term/section/subject/enrollment/student parents. Raw tombstones are retained for validation. Existing annual attendance/charts, score calculations, templates, context, photos and local archive formats are preserved.

For account sources, every new source and required parent must explicitly match the mounted claimed workspace owner. Ownerless legacy/local records are deliberately omitted: this slice does not treat an arbitrary teaching ID or a guessed receipt as ownership proof. Unclaimed/legacy workspaces are omitted entirely. Ownerless Demo records are accepted only in the exact `demo` / `rakeezx-demo` workspace. No real account was accessed or mutated.

Both section and term must lead to the selected year. Dated goals/logs must fall inside valid saved term start/end dates, at or before the current as-of date, and match an existing held, nondeleted session. Missing saved term bounds cause omission rather than guessing calendar dates. Missing/mismatched sessions, future/undated/cancelled/scheduled rows, foreign owners, deleted sources/parents, malformed arrangements and invalid memberships are omitted. Archived setups are omitted; historical dated logs/goals survive only through a valid section archive date. Closed-term setups are omitted. Identical IDs count once; conflicting duplicates, including a duplicate tombstone, fail closed.

Descriptions and metrics use fixed aggregate text only. All source references remain directly accessible internally but are non-enumerable, so spreading/serializing suggestions does not disclose source IDs (including log IDs which can contain student identities). The view never renders references. The three new sources also print a fixed, uneditable provenance notice independent of the teacher-editable description. Templates retain their existing distinct provenance and inclusion rules.
